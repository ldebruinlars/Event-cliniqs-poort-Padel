Fifteen seconds, vertical 9:16, a fast, punchy cartoon trailer in six shots with hard cuts on the beat. Every shot has a moving camera; nothing is ever static.

REFERENCES. The first reference is the pumpkin-head padel player (character sheet). The second reference is the friendly ghost padel player (character sheet). The third reference is the haunted padel court with the skeleton crowd in the stands, lightning, blowing leaves, swirling green fog and floating lanterns: this living, moving environment is the setting of every shot. The fourth reference is the glowing lime padel ball with a ghost face and how it squashes on impact.

SHOT 1, 0 to 1.5 seconds, THE HOOK. Pitch-black extreme close-up. A lightning flash, and the carved eyes and grin of the pumpkin-head ignite neon lime, flames bursting from the stem. Fast push-in.

SHOT 2, 1.5 to 4 seconds. FPV drone shot racing low over the court from the near baseline toward the net, through swirling green fog and whirling orange leaves. The skeleton crowd in the stands jumps and waves pumpkin flags, lanterns bob, string lights swing. On the far side the ghost spins its racket and grins.

SHOT 3, 4 to 7.5 seconds, THE RALLY. Side-on tracking shot, the camera sliding along the net with the ball. The pumpkin-head is on the left half of the court, the ghost on the right half, facing each other across the net. Quick rally: the ghost hits, the pumpkin-head volleys, the ghost stretches and lobs, the ball with its ghost face bounces off the glass wall. The jack-o'-lanterns along the walls turn their faces to follow the ball. Every hit throws up a puff of leaves.

SHOT 4, 7.5 to 10 seconds, THE SMASH. Low angle, slow motion. The pumpkin-head leaps high against the full moon, lightning cracking behind him, cape whipping. The camera orbits 180 degrees around him in bullet time. The centre of the racket meets the ball dead on: the ball squashes flat and a huge comic starburst of orange and lime sparks explodes from the impact. This impact must be clearly visible.

SHOT 5, 10 to 12 seconds. The ball blasts past the ghost in a lime streak. The ghost poofs into a puff of white mist and a shockwave of fog rolls across the court. The skeleton crowd erupts, bones and confetti flying, and a swarm of bats bursts into the sky. Whip pan.

SHOT 6, 12 to 15 seconds, THE HERO. Medium shot facing the pumpkin-head. He walks toward the camera through the fog, stops and points his racket straight at the lens, grin and eyes glowing lime, flames crackling. For the last two seconds he holds the pose and the camera settles, with calm dark space above his head and below his chest.

IDENTITY. Both characters stay exactly as in their reference sheets in every shot: the pumpkin-head with the same carved grin and triangle eyes, black sports shirt with the small orange pumpkin emblem, black shorts, lime wristbands, orange-and-black tattered cape and black racket with lime dots; the ghost with its white sheet, big oval eyes, purple headband and wristbands and white racket with purple dots.

STYLE. Bold comic halftone cartoon exactly like the references: thick ink outlines, cel shading, halftone dots, palette black, deep purple, dark blue, pumpkin orange and neon lime. Never photorealistic. Spooky but fun and family friendly. No text, letters, logos or subtitles anywhere.

AUDIO. Driving spooky surf-rock with drums, organ and a theremin hook, cuts landing on the beat. Thunder on the lightning, whoosh on the drone flight, a hollow "pok" on every hit, a huge punchy impact on the smash, a cheeky ghost "wooo", the skeleton crowd rattling and cheering. No dialogue, no voice-over.
