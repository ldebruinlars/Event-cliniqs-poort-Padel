Fifteen seconds, vertical 9:16, cartoon animation. One continuous shot, no cuts, the camera moves smoothly the whole time.

REFERENCES. The first reference is the pumpkin-head padel player (character sheet, four views). The second reference is the friendly ghost padel player (character sheet, four views). The third reference is the haunted padel court at night, which is the setting for the whole video. The fourth reference is the glowing lime padel ball with a little ghost face, including how it squashes on impact.

COURT GEOMETRY. Hold this layout throughout. The net crosses the middle of the frame. The pumpkin-head player is on the NEAR side, seen from behind and three-quarter, facing the net. The ghost is on the FAR side beyond the net, FACING THE CAMERA and therefore facing the pumpkin-head player. The ball travels back and forth along the line between them. Both characters always watch the ball. Never let both look the same direction.

SECONDS 0-2.5. The camera glides low over the near baseline through rolling fog toward the net. Huge full moon behind the far glass wall, bats flapping past, jack-o'-lanterns flickering along the glass walls, orange string lights swaying. The court is still empty.

SECONDS 2.5-4.5. The ghost rises up out of the fog on the far side, twirls its racket, wiggles its sheet and grins at the camera.

SECONDS 4.5-6.5. The pumpkin-head player drops into frame from above on the near side, orange-and-black cape flaring, lands in a crouch with his back to the camera, the lime flames on his pumpkin head flaring up.

SECONDS 6.5-9. The ghost serves the glowing ghost-face ball over the net. It bounces once on the blue floor, kicks off the side glass wall with a little ghostly "boo" face, and floats up high on the pumpkin-head's side.

SECONDS 9-10.5. THE CONTACT. The pumpkin-head leaps, cape spread, and smashes. The centre of the racket meets the ball dead on: the ball squashes flat and a big comic starburst of orange and lime sparks explodes from the impact point. This impact must be clearly visible and unmistakable.

SECONDS 10.5-12. The ball rockets back over the net in a lime streak. The ghost spins around in surprise, poofs into a puff of white mist, then reappears laughing, while a swarm of bats bursts up into the moonlight.

SECONDS 12-15. The camera swings around in front of the pumpkin-head player to a medium shot, facing him. He lands, points his racket straight at the camera, eyes and grin glowing lime, flames crackling. For the final two seconds he holds that pose and the camera holds still, with calm dark space above his head and below his chest.

IDENTITY. Both characters stay exactly as in their reference sheets in every frame: the pumpkin-head keeps the same carved grin and triangle eyes, black sports shirt with the small orange pumpkin emblem, black shorts, lime wristbands, orange-and-black tattered cape and black racket with lime dots; the ghost keeps its white sheet, big oval eyes, purple headband and wristbands and white racket with purple dots.

STYLE. Bold comic halftone cartoon exactly like the references: thick clean ink outlines, cel-shaded colour, halftone dot shading, palette black, deep purple, dark blue, pumpkin orange and neon lime. The cartoon look stays constant from first frame to last, never drifting toward photorealism. Spooky but fun and family friendly. No text, no letters, no logos, no subtitles on screen.

AUDIO. Playful spooky Halloween music with surf-rock guitar and organ, whooshes, a hollow "pok" on every ball hit, a big punchy impact on the smash, a cheeky ghost "wooo" and flapping bats. No dialogue, no voice-over.
